Goa is the easiest beach holiday from anywhere in India – sunny beaches in the north, quiet resorts in the south, Portuguese churches in Old Goa and great seafood and vegetarian food alike.

Best time to visit Goa

November–February for beaches and nightlife; monsoon (June–September) for greenery and quiet resorts.

Goa – frequently asked questions

North Goa or South Goa?

North Goa is lively with beaches, markets and nightlife; South Goa is quieter with cleaner beaches and luxury resorts.

Is Goa good in the monsoon?

Yes for a relaxed, green holiday with lower prices; sea activities stop from June to September.

Can you arrange Jain food in Goa?

Yes – we choose hotels that can serve Jain and pure vegetarian meals.
