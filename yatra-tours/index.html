Yatra Tour Packages

A yatra is a journey of faith, and it deserves careful planning. We organise comfortable, well-paced yatras for families, senior citizens and groups, taking care of travel, stays, darshan planning and food.

What we offer

  • Char Dham Yatra – Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, Badrinath
  • Vaishno Devi and Amarnath Yatra
  • Kashi Vishwanath, Prayagraj and Ayodhya
  • Shirdi, Trimbakeshwar and Jyotirlinga tours
  • Dwarka, Somnath and Gujarat pilgrimages
  • Jain tirth yatras – Palitana, Shikharji, Girnar, Ranakpur

Why book with us

We plan yatras with senior citizens in mind – sensible daily distances, clean stays and help with helicopter, pony or palki bookings where available.

Wherever you are in India, plan everything with us over call and WhatsApp.
