🛕 Yatra Tours

Yatra & Pilgrimage Tour Packages from Mumbai

A yatra is a journey of faith, and it deserves careful planning. We organise comfortable, well-paced pilgrimage tours for families, senior citizens and groups, taking care of travel, stays, darshan planning and food.

💬 Get a free plan on WhatsApp📞 98431 21212

What we offer

  • Char Dham Yatra – Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, Badrinath
  • Vaishno Devi and Amarnath Yatra
  • Kashi Vishwanath, Prayagraj and Ayodhya
  • Shirdi, Trimbakeshwar and Jyotirlinga tours
  • Dwarka, Somnath and Gujarat pilgrimages
  • Jain tirth yatras – Palitana, Shikharji, Girnar, Ranakpur

Why book with us

We plan yatras with senior citizens in mind – sensible daily distances, clean stays and help with helicopter, pony or palki bookings where available.

Visit our office in Mulund West, Mumbai (open daily 9 am – 10 pm), or plan everything over call and WhatsApp. We serve travellers across Mulund, Thane, Bhandup, Nahur, Powai, Ghatkopar and the rest of Mumbai.
