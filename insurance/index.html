Claim help

Something went wrong on your trip? Here's what to do

If you bought your policy through us, we help you with the claim from start to finish.

🏥 Medical emergency

  • Call the insurer's 24×7 assistance number printed on your policy before going to hospital, if you can
  • Ask for a cashless admission at a network hospital
  • Keep every bill, prescription and report

🧳 Lost or delayed baggage

  • Report it at the airline counter before leaving the airport
  • Get the Property Irregularity Report (PIR)
  • Keep receipts for essentials you buy

🛂 Lost passport

  • File a police report where it was lost
  • Contact the nearest Indian Embassy / Consulate
  • Keep copies of the police report and new travel document

✈️ Delay or cancellation

  • Ask the airline for a written delay / cancellation letter
  • Keep boarding passes and new tickets
  • Inform the insurer as soon as possible
