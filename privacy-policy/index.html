Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

The Real Tours and Travels respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Name, phone number, email address and booking details you share with us by call, WhatsApp, email, our website or in person.
  • Traveller details and documents (such as passport copies) needed to make bookings and visa applications.
  • Payment information is collected and processed by our payment partner Razorpay; we do not store card or bank details.

How we use your information

  • To plan and book your travel, apply for visas and insurance, and contact you about your trip.
  • To send payment receipts and service updates.
  • To share travel offers, only if you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time.

Sharing

We share your details only with airlines, hotels, insurers, embassies/visa centres and other service providers as needed for your booking, or when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for your bookings and legal requirements.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us using the details below.
