🇪🇺 Europe (Schengen)

Appointment at the visa centre with biometrics

PriceRequest Price

Documents required

  • Original passport valid for at least 6 months from the return date, with at least 2 blank pages
  • Copies of old passports, if any
  • 2 recent photos, 35 × 45 mm, white background (80% face)
  • Covering letter explaining the purpose of travel
  • Bank statements for the last 6 months (signed and stamped by the bank)
  • Income tax returns (ITR) for the last 2–3 years
  • Salaried: salary slips (last 3 months) and leave/NOC letter from employer
  • Self-employed: business proof (GST certificate / Shop Act / company registration)
  • Students: school or college ID and bonafide letter; retired: pension proof
  • Confirmed return flight booking
  • Hotel bookings for the whole stay
  • Day-wise travel itinerary
  • Travel insurance covering the whole Schengen stay, minimum €30,000 medical cover

Requirements can change and may differ for business, student or family visits. We confirm the final checklist for your case before you apply.
