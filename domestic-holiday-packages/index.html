Domestic Holiday Packages from Mumbai

India has a holiday for every family, every season and every budget. From the snow of Kashmir and Manali to the backwaters of Kerala and the beaches of Goa and the Andamans, we plan complete domestic holidays from Mumbai with flights or trains, hotels, private cab transfers and sightseeing – so all you have to do is pack.

What we offer

  • Kashmir – Srinagar houseboats, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg
  • Himachal – Manali, Shimla, Dharamshala and the Atal Tunnel
  • Kerala – Munnar, Thekkady, Alleppey houseboats and Kovalam
  • Rajasthan – Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur and Jaisalmer
  • Goa, Andaman, Darjeeling–Gangtok, Ladakh and the North East
  • Weekend getaways near Mumbai – Lonavala, Mahabaleshwar, Matheran

Why book with us

Every itinerary is checked for travel time, kid- and senior-friendly pacing and good-quality family hotels. You get one point of contact who is on call throughout the trip.

Visit our office in Mulund West, Mumbai (open daily 9 am – 10 pm), or plan everything over call and WhatsApp. We serve travellers across Mulund, Thane, Bhandup, Nahur, Powai, Ghatkopar and the rest of Mumbai.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best domestic holiday for a family from Mumbai in winter?

December–February is ideal for snow in Kashmir and Manali, and for pleasant weather in Kerala, Goa and Rajasthan. Call us and we will suggest the best fit for your dates and family.

Do your packages include flights or trains from Mumbai?

Yes. We can include flights or train tickets from Mumbai, or plan land-only packages if you prefer to book travel yourself.

Can you arrange Jain or vegetarian food on domestic tours?

Yes. We regularly arrange pure vegetarian and Jain food, and offer special Jain yatra tours with our own Maharaj.
