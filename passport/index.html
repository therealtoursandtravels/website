🆕 Fresh Passport (Adult)

Online application, then appointment at the Passport Seva Kendra and police verification. Carry originals plus one set of self-attested copies

Government fee₹2,500
36 pages · ₹3,500 for 60 pages
Our service charge₹1,000 per form · incl. GST
Apply / Enquire

Total per form = government fee (no GST) + our service charge (GST included).

Documents required

  • Proof of present address (any one): Aadhaar / e-Aadhaar, Voter ID, electricity / water / telephone / gas bill, bank passbook or account statement with photo, income tax assessment order, registered rent agreement, or your spouse's passport (if your address is the same)
  • Proof of date of birth (any one): birth certificate from the Registrar of Births and Deaths / Municipal Corporation, school leaving / transfer / matriculation certificate, PAN card, driving licence, Voter ID, LIC or public-company policy bond, or service record / pay pension order (government employees and pensioners)
  • Aadhaar is accepted as address proof, but not as proof of date of birth under the 2025 passport rules
  • 10th marksheet or higher qualification certificate, if you have one – for non-ECR status
  • Name change documents (gazette notification or marriage certificate), if your name differs across documents
  • Government / PSU employees: Identity Certificate (Annexure A) or No Objection Certificate (Annexure B) from your office
  • Appointment confirmation (we book it and fill the application for you)
  • Your name, date of birth and parents' names should match on every document – we check this before your appointment

Requirements are set by Passport Seva (Ministry of External Affairs) and can change. We confirm the final checklist for your case before you apply.

👶 Passport for Minors (below 18) – both parents

Both parents give consent by signing the declaration (Annexure D). Ideally both parents come to the appointment with the child

Government fee₹1,750
36 pages
Our service charge₹1,000 per form · incl. GST
Apply / Enquire

Total per form = government fee (no GST) + our service charge (GST included).

Documents required

  • Birth certificate of the child – for children born on or after 1 October 2023 this is the only accepted proof of date of birth
  • Children born before 1 October 2023 may instead use another accepted proof, such as a school leaving certificate
  • Proof of present address in the name of either parent
  • Self-attested copies of both parents' passports (first two and last two pages), if they hold passports
  • Annexure D – declaration signed by both parents (we prepare it)
  • If the child's name is to be printed with a surname different from the parents', supporting documents – we advise

Requirements are set by Passport Seva (Ministry of External Affairs) and can change. We confirm the final checklist for your case before you apply.

👩‍👦 Minor – Single Parent / One Parent Unavailable

A child's passport can still be issued when only one parent can apply – the documents depend on the situation. We prepare the right declaration for your case

Government fee₹1,750
36 pages
Our service charge₹1,000 per form · incl. GST
Apply / Enquire

Total per form = government fee (no GST) + our service charge (GST included).

Documents required

  • Birth certificate of the child – for children born on or after 1 October 2023 this is the only accepted proof of date of birth
  • Proof of present address and passport copy (if any) of the parent who is applying
  • One parent has passed away: Annexure D signed by the surviving parent, plus the death certificate of the other parent
  • Divorced or separated: Annexure C – declaration by the applying parent, plus a copy of the divorce decree or custody order, if one exists. If custody is being disputed in court, the court's permission may be needed
  • Unmarried / single parent, or the other parent's whereabouts are unknown: Annexure C – declaration by the applying parent. Only one parent's name needs to be printed on the passport
  • Other parent gives consent but cannot come: declaration signed by both parents (Annexure D)
  • Legal guardian applying: guardianship order from the court plus Annexure D
  • Annexure formats are updated from time to time – we always use the current format from Passport Seva and confirm the exact documents for your case

Requirements are set by Passport Seva (Ministry of External Affairs) and can change. We confirm the final checklist for your case before you apply.
