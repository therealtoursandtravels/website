Why Travel Insurance Matters: A Simple Guide for Mumbai Travellers

By The Real Tours and Travels · Mulund West, Mumbai · 23 November 2026

Travel insurance is the one thing most travellers skip – until something goes wrong. A medical emergency abroad or a lost bag can turn a dream holiday into a stressful one. Here is what you should know, in simple words.

What does travel insurance usually cover?

  • Medical emergencies abroad – hospitalisation and treatment, which can be very expensive in many countries.
  • Emergency evacuation and repatriation.
  • Trip cancellation or interruption for covered reasons.
  • Lost or delayed baggage and loss of passport.
  • Flight delays and missed connections, depending on the plan.
  • Personal liability in some plans.

Every policy is different, so always read what is covered, the sum insured and the exclusions.

When is it mandatory?

Some visas require travel insurance. For example, a Schengen visa for Europe requires medical insurance covering the whole stay in the Schengen area. Some other countries and cruise lines also ask for proof of insurance. We check this for you when we plan your trip.

Who needs it most?

  • Senior citizens – medical cover is the most important protection.
  • Families with children – small illnesses abroad can still mean a doctor or hospital visit.
  • Adventure and snow trips – check that activities like skiing are covered.
  • Students and business travellers on longer stays.

Tips before you buy

  • Buy insurance as soon as you book, so cancellation cover starts early.
  • Declare pre-existing medical conditions honestly.
  • Save the insurer's emergency helpline number and your policy on your phone.
  • Keep bills and reports – you will need them for any claim.

Frequently asked questions

Is travel insurance mandatory for international travel?

It is mandatory for some visas, such as the Schengen visa for Europe, and strongly recommended for every international trip.

Does travel insurance cover senior citizens?

Yes, plans are available for senior citizens. Pre-existing conditions must be declared, and cover varies by plan.

When should I buy travel insurance?

As soon as you book your trip, so that trip cancellation cover starts early.
