Passport and Visa Checklist for Indian Travellers

By The Real Tours and Travels · Mulund West, Mumbai · 23 November 2026

Visa trouble is the most common reason international holidays get delayed. A little planning avoids most of it. Here is the checklist we go through with every family before they travel abroad.

1. Check your passport first

  • Validity: most countries want your passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date.
  • Blank pages: keep at least two blank pages for visas and stamps.
  • Condition: a damaged or water-stained passport can be refused at immigration.
  • Children's passports are valid for a shorter period – check them separately.

If you need a new passport or a renewal, apply well before you book. We help you fill the application, prepare documents and book the appointment at the Passport Seva Kendra.

2. Know which type of visa you need

Depending on the destination, Indian passport holders may need a regular visa applied in advance, an e-visa applied online, or a visa on arrival – and some countries allow visa-free entry for short stays. These rules change often, so we always check the latest requirements for your exact travel dates before you book.

3. Common documents for a tourist visa

  • Passport with required validity, and old passports if you have them
  • Recent photographs in the size the embassy specifies
  • Confirmed or tentative flight and hotel bookings, and a day-wise itinerary
  • Bank statements, salary slips or ITR as proof of funds
  • Leave letter from employer or business registration documents
  • Travel insurance – mandatory for some visas, such as Schengen visas for Europe
  • For minors: birth certificate and consent from parents where required

4. Apply early – especially for Europe, UK and USA

Appointment slots for Schengen, UK and US visas can be booked out for weeks, especially before summer holidays and December. As a rule of thumb, start the process at least two to three months before travel, and earlier for the USA.

5. After the visa

  • Check that the name, passport number and dates on the visa are correct.
  • Carry printouts of your visa, bookings and insurance as well as copies on your phone.
  • Keep a photocopy of your passport separately from the original.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How long should my passport be valid to travel abroad?

Most countries require your passport to be valid for at least six months beyond your return date, with blank pages for stamps.

How early should I apply for a Schengen or UK visa?

Start two to three months before travel. Appointment slots fill up quickly before peak holiday seasons.

Do you help with passport renewal in Mumbai?

Yes. We help with new passports and renewals – forms, documents and booking the Passport Seva Kendra appointment.
